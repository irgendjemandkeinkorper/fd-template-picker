Recommended pass criteria
1. Record source URL,page ID,actual viewport,DPR,scrollY,heading/link/form/menu signatures and bounds together per capture after content settles; verify no window resize during collection.
2. Prefer viewport tiles with fresh actual scroll positions. When full-page capture is used, compare top/middle/bottom image patches to independent settled viewport references at known scroll positions and reject scaling/repetition mismatches.
3. Validate crop dimensions, full coverage, per-image content identity and semantic identity separately. Geometry alone must never mark semantic/visual review complete.
4. Navigation: visible, readable labels in output or open mobile menu state plus a working menu trigger, links in bounds, no unexpected clipping. Include native zoom/large-view in picker for desktop images reduced into cards.
5. Food: visible heading, at least one actual menu item/category or intentional menu CTA, no hero/nav inside crop. Contact: expected field labels. Membership/private parties: expected heading/body/CTA visible. Source selector and published ID must match crop.
6. Review all72 required screenshots as a labeled contact sheet then open questionable images at native scale; maintain explicit reviewed/failed/pending status. Invalid screenshots should not count as captured coverage.
